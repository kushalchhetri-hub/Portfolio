Choosing Science in +2 is my first deliberate step toward that dream — and toward serving my country through medicine or engineering.

I aspire to become a doctor — specifically a neurosurgeon or a public health specialist. Nepal needs more dedicated healthcare professionals, and I want to be part of the generation that transforms our nation's healthcare system.
